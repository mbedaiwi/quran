# Apple App Store Compliance Checklist (iOS)

Use this before submission in App Store Connect.

- [ ] App only loads HTTPS resources.
- [ ] `NSAllowsArbitraryLoads` is `false`.
- [ ] Associated Domains configured for universal links.
- [ ] Privacy Policy URL is provided and publicly accessible.
- [ ] App Privacy questionnaire matches actual SDK/data behavior.
- [ ] Age rating and content declarations reflect Quran app audience.
- [ ] No misleading metadata, screenshots, or claims.
- [ ] If analytics/crash SDKs are added, update privacy declarations and policy.
- [ ] Sign in/purchases (if any) comply with Apple rules.
- [ ] Export compliance and encryption questions answered correctly.
