# Google Play Compliance Checklist (Android)

Use this before rollout in Play Console.

- [ ] App targets latest required target SDK.
- [ ] HTTPS-only policy enforced (`usesCleartextTraffic=false`).
- [ ] Digital Asset Links hosted and verified for app links.
- [ ] Data safety form matches production behavior.
- [ ] Privacy Policy URL is publicly accessible.
- [ ] Ads declaration is accurate.
- [ ] Content rating questionnaire completed accurately.
- [ ] No deceptive behavior or hidden functionality.
- [ ] Background permissions are not requested unless required and disclosed.
- [ ] Sensitive permissions are justified and documented.
